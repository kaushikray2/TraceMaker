Detailed Description

RAII claim of secondary worker slots under the cap.

secondary_allowance() alone is a read, not a reservation: concurrent secondary stages (zone refill runs inside every portfolio variant's setup when the board has unfilled zones) would each spend the same headroom and oversubscribe the cap. A claim atomically takes as many of want as the cap leaves free after the live primary workers and other live claims, and releases them on scope exit. With no cap set, count() is -1: unbounded, the historical behaviour.
