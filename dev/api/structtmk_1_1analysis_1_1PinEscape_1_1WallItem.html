Detailed Description

The walling items that are routed copper (tracks, arcs, vias), identified by board index: what a rip-up may remove to free the pin.

Fixed copper (pads, zones, graphics, holes, the board edge) is never listed. Sorted by (kind, index), duplicates removed.
